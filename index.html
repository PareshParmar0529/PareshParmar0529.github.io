About me
A decade of building software that matters

In 2015, with a vision to transform the landscape of financial cooperation and empower communities, Paresh Parmar founded Apex Web Infotech. What began as a humble startup has grown into one of Gujarat's most trusted software companies.

With over 10 years of experience in software development and entrepreneurship, Paresh leads a team of passionate developers building cutting-edge solutions for cooperative credit societies, financial institutions, and businesses across India.

His expertise spans web development, mobile applications, and enterprise software — with a deep specialization in cooperative society management systems trusted by 500+ clients across Gujarat and India.

"Our mission is to pioneer digital transformation through innovative web and mobile development — uniting technology and purpose to empower businesses and communities."
